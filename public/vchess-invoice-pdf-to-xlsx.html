V.Chess 發票 PDF → Excel

大量選取或拖放 PDF；支援一頁兩張發票（左右欄）。Excel 僅輸出 11 欄：學員姓名、學員編號、老師、課程名、時段、日期列、每堂單價、數量、該行總額、發票號、發票日。
Bulk PDFs; two invoices per page. XLSX has 11 columns only (name, ID, teacher, lesson, time, dates, unit price, qty, line total, invoice no., date).

      注意 · Notes
    
      僅支援內含可選取文字的 PDF。多頁檔逐頁處理；一頁兩張（左右欄）時會用座標分欄並以 No.: / To: 再切分。掃描圖需 OCR。
    
      Text PDFs. Two invoices per sheet: column split + No.: / To: segments. Scanned PDFs need OCR.

      Open via your dev server, not file://, so the PDF.js worker loads correctly.
